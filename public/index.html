Your Role

You're the owner. Not the manager.

This isn't Football Manager. You don't pick the team or shout from the touchline. You run the club — the money, the staff, the infrastructure, the politics. Set the strategy and direction. Your staff execute it.

YOU CONTROL
  • Capital allocation & financial decisions
  • Facility investment & infrastructure build-out
  • Staff hiring across 6 distinct roles

  • Agent & guardian relations
  • Club identity, kit colours & strategic direction

Delegate everything to your DoF and Manager and focus on the boardroom — or override every decision yourself. The level of involvement is always your choice. Promotion means your decisions worked, not just your tactics.

Game Systems

Every angle of running a football club. In your pocket.

From scouting raw talent to negotiating investor equity — the full ownership experience is here.

Transfer Market

Buy, sell, and loan players with agent-negotiated fees, guardian approval, and real market pressure. NPC clubs build squads and bid on your players too.

Director of Football

Hire a DoF and delegate the day-to-day. Toggle auto-scouting, auto-signing, auto-selling, and auto-repairs. The key to a hands-off ownership approach — or the first role you override when you want control back.

Full Staff Management

Hire from 6 distinct roles — Coach, Manager, Director of Football, Facility Manager, Chairman, and Scout. Each shapes your club's ceiling. The right hire at the right time changes everything.

Finance & Investment

Negotiate sponsor contracts, accept investor equity stakes, and manage wages and upkeep. Capital allocation is the core ownership decision — every pound spent is a bet on what your club needs most.

Facilities & Infrastructure

Three investment tracks with distinct purposes. TRAINING (technical zone, speed track, conditioning suites) accelerates player development. MEDICAL (physio clinic, medical suite) cuts injuries and protects squad depth. STADIUM & COMMERCIAL upgrades grow matchday income and reputation — unlocking better sponsors and higher-quality recruits. Every pound spent is a choice about the kind of club you're building.

Competition Hub

Track your league table, fixtures, golden boot standings, and browse every division in the pyramid. Full season history and trophies recorded forever in the museum.

Match Events Engine

Goals, assists, star performances, and off-days fire narrative events mid-match that directly shift player morale. Every match tells a story.

Fan Happiness System

Five happiness tiers that respond to results, signings, promotions, and trophies. Permanent fan events record your greatest moments forever.

Tactics & Club Identity

Set your formation and playing style — Possession, Direct, Counter, or High Press — and customise your kit colours. Delegate to your Manager or set it yourself.

Player Archetypes

Every player has a type. Learn to read them.

Archetypes are derived from a player's 8-trait personality matrix. They shape how players behave — in training, in the dressing room, and in big matches.

The Prospect

Raw talent with sky-high potential. Handle carefully — push too hard and they crack.

The Maverick

Driven by personal glory. Can light up a game or derail a dressing room — often both in the same week.

The Iceman

Cool under pressure with elite consistency. Quiet, professional, and invaluable in a promotion run.

The Journeyman

Experienced and adaptable. Won't set the world on fire but will never let you down either.

The Leader

High loyalty and natural authority. Mentors younger players and lifts the squad when morale dips.

The Grinder

Maximum determination, minimal flair. Does the unglamorous work week after week without complaint.

The Wildcard

Unpredictable and ego-driven. Brilliant in flashes. A constant headache for the coaching staff.

The Veteran

Experience beyond their years. Slowing down, but their presence steadies the whole squad.

The Simulation

Deep systems. Real decisions.

This isn't a clicker. Every week brings real ownership decisions — where to invest, who to hire, how to react to your players' personalities and the narratives unfolding in your dressing room.


An 8-trait personality matrix governs player behaviour. Social graphs track relationships and trigger chained narrative events. Guardians push back. Agents negotiate. Your Director of Football has an opinion. Investors want returns. It's all connected.

  • 01
    8-Trait Personality Matrix Determination, professionalism, ambition, loyalty, adaptability, pressure, temperament, consistency — each trait evolves over time and shapes player behaviour.
  • 02
    Social Graph & Chained Events Player relationships form dynamically. Arguments, rivalries, and alliances chain into follow-up events that reshape your squad's chemistry week after week.
  • 03
    Guardian & Agent Dynamics Young players have guardians with loyalty scores and demand levels. Agents push transfers. Manage relationships or lose your best talent to the highest bidder.
  • 04
    Dynamic NPC Market NPC clubs actively manage rosters and bid on players — including yours. Player valuations shift with age, ability, and contract status in real time.
  • 05
    Player Archetypes Eight archetypes derived from personality traits. Each has a scout-written report and distinct behavioural tendencies in training and big matches.
  • 06
    Manager Personality Your managerial temperament, discipline, and ambition shift based on decisions made. The board notices. The players notice. Win rates affect your job security.
  • 07
    Full Match Reports Every match generates a detailed inbox report with individual player ratings, goals, assists, match events, and a team average. Know exactly how you won or lost.
  • 08
    Trophy Museum & Season History Every title, promotion, and golden boot is recorded forever in the Museum. Season-by-season stats stored in SQLite — your full career, always intact.
  • 09
    Club Finances & Investment Negotiate equity deals with investors, renew or void sponsor contracts, and allocate capital across wages, facilities, and transfers. Every financial decision determines how fast your club can grow — and how exposed you are when things go wrong.
How it Works

One tap. One week. Infinite consequences.

The Weekly Tick engine runs everything on-device — no internet needed mid-game. When you're ready, sync to the cloud leaderboard.

Make Your Decisions

Manage transfers, allocate budget to facility upgrades, respond to inbox messages from agents, sponsors, investors, and your board. React to guardian demands. Delegate what you don't want to handle yourself.

Advance the Week

The engine simulates your match with live match events, processes wages, applies facility benefits to player development, runs upkeep, fires narrative incidents — all offline, all on-device.

React to Results

Read the full match report, check your league position, accept or reject transfer bids. Player morale shifts. Fan happiness reacts. The NPC market moves.

Build Your Legacy

Climb the pyramid. Win your first title. Hit the Hall of Fame leaderboard. Every promotion is recorded forever. Or get relegated. No pressure.

The League System

Eight tiers. One goal. Get to the top.

Every new club starts in the bottom tier. 22 clubs per division. A full 42-game season — home and away against everyone. Promotion spots are earned, not given.


Browse every league in the pyramid from the Competition Hub. NPC clubs have fully simulated squads, staff, finances, and tactics. Beating them means building something real.

Privacy Policy

Overview

Build My Club ("we", "us", "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights in relation to it.

Information We Collect

When you register an account, we collect your email address and a password (stored as a secure hash). We do not collect your name, phone number, or payment information.

During normal gameplay, your save data is stored locally on your device. If you choose to sync your progress, we receive aggregate gameplay statistics (such as your club's reputation and career earnings) to maintain leaderboard rankings.

How We Use Your Information

Your email address is used solely to authenticate your account and, if you opt in, to send you updates about the game. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes.

Gameplay sync data is used exclusively to calculate and display global leaderboard rankings. We retain sync records to detect and prevent cheating.

Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored on secured servers. Passwords are hashed using industry-standard algorithms and are never stored in plain text. We use HTTPS for all data in transit.

Data Retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us.

Third-Party Services

The game is offline-first and does not integrate third-party advertising or analytics SDKs. This website uses Google Fonts, which may set cookies in accordance with Google's own privacy policy.

Your Rights

Depending on where you are located, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete the personal data we hold about you. To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at the address below.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the date at the bottom of this page. Continued use of the game or this website after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
