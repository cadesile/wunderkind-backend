Game Systems

Every angle of football management. In your pocket.

From scouting raw talent to setting your Director of Football loose — the full game is here.

Transfer Market

Buy, sell, and loan players with agent-negotiated fees, guardian dynamics, and real market pressure. Every deal has consequences.

Full Staff Management

Hire from 6 distinct roles — Coach, Manager, Director of Football, Facility Manager, Chairman, and Scout. Each shapes your club's ceiling.

Fixture Scheduling

Full double round-robin season with alternating home and away fixtures. Results ripple through league tables, morale, and fan happiness in real time.

Facilities & Infrastructure

Upgrade your training pitches, medical suites, tactical rooms, and stands. Facilities directly affect player development and matchday income.

Finance & Investment

Negotiate sponsor deals, accept investor equity, manage wages and upkeep. Keep the books balanced or risk the board pulling the plug.

Fan Happiness System

Fans react to every match result, signing, and upgrade. Happy fans drive income. Angry fans demand change. You need to keep them onside.

Director of Football

Hire a DoF and delegate the day-to-day. Auto-manage player events, auto-renew contracts, auto-assign scouts. Hands-on or hands-off — your call.

Competition Hub

Track your league table, fixtures, golden boot standings, and browse every division in the pyramid. Full season history records your journey.

Tactics & Club Identity

Choose your formation, set your playing style — Possession, Direct, Counter, or High Press — and customise your kit colours and club badge.

The Simulation

Deep systems. Real decisions.

This isn't a clicker. Every week brings real choices — who plays, who to sign, where to invest, how to react to your players' personalities and the narratives unfolding in your dressing room.


An 8-trait personality matrix governs player behaviour. Social graphs track relationships and trigger chained narrative events. Guardians push back. Agents negotiate. Your Director of Football has an opinion. It's all connected.

  • 01
    8-Trait Personality Matrix Determination, professionalism, ambition, loyalty, adaptability, pressure, temperament, consistency — each trait evolves over time.
  • 02
    Social Graph & Narrative Events Player relationships form dynamically. Arguments, rivalries, and alliances chain into follow-up events that shape your squad's chemistry.
  • 03
    Guardian & Agent Dynamics Young players have guardians with loyalty scores and demand levels. Agents push transfers. Manage relationships or lose your best talent.
  • 04
    Director of Football Hire a DoF with their own archetype and philosophy. Toggle auto-management for scouting, contracts, signings, and player events.
  • 05
    Manager Personality Your managerial temperament, discipline, and ambition shift based on decisions made. The board notices. The players notice.
  • 06
    Player Archetypes Players are classified into archetypes — The Prospect, The Maverick, The Iceman and more. Each has a scout-written report and distinct behavioural tendencies.
  • 07
    Full Match Reports Every match generates a detailed inbox report with individual player ratings, goals, assists, and a team average. Know exactly how you won or lost.
  • 08
    Season History Track your journey season-by-season. League finishes, promotion runs, golden boot winners — the full record of your club's rise.
How it Works

One tap. One week. Infinite consequences.

The Weekly Tick engine runs everything on-device — no internet needed mid-game. When you're ready, sync to the cloud leaderboard.

Make Your Decisions

Set your formation, playing style, manage transfers, respond to inbox messages from agents, sponsors, and your board.

Advance the Week

The game engine simulates your match, processes wages, runs facility upkeep, and fires narrative events — all offline.

React to Results

Read the full match report, check your league position, accept or reject transfer bids. Every outcome shapes the next week.

Build Your Legacy

Climb the league pyramid. Grow your reputation. Hit the Hall of Fame leaderboard. Or get relegated. No pressure.

The League System

Eight tiers. One goal. Get to the top.

Every new club starts in the bottom tier. 22 clubs per division. A full 42-game season — home and away against everyone. Promotion spots are earned, not given.


Browse every league in the pyramid from the Competition Hub. NPC clubs are fully simulated — squads, staff, finances, tactics. Beating them means building something real.
